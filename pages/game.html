Spoiler-light playthrough guide

A clear route for a first complete playthrough of God of War.

  1. Start with the story pathComplete The Marked Trees and follow the objective markers until the Lake of Nine opens.
  2. Clear side routes as they appearAfter each major story chapter, explore newly available shores, favour quests and locked paths before moving on.
  3. Prepare before the summitUpgrade one armour set, keep resurrection stones stocked, and finish optional favours you want before the late-game return journey.
  4. Finish the main route, then clean upUse the post-story world state to revisit regions for ravens, artefacts, valkyries and remaining trophies.
