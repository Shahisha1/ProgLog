Data Sovereignty

Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: August 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Local Storage Privacy

Proglog is built from the ground up on the principle of data sovereignty. Your game checklists, unlocked trophies, custom notes, and hunter profiles are stored directly within your browser's client-side localStorage. No personal trophy data or gameplay logs are transmitted to or stored on remote tracking servers without your explicit consent.

2. Zero Third-Party Cookies & Telemetry

We do not use invasive tracking pixels, ad network beacons, or behavioral analytics cookies. Your browsing habits and gaming library remain entirely private to your device.

3. Data Export & Backup Rights

You have full ownership of your data at all times. You can export your entire trophy library to an unencrypted .json backup file at any time from your Profile page, and restore it on another device or browser when needed.

4. Authentication (Google & GitHub)

When authenticating via Google or GitHub, Proglog uses public profile metadata (display name and avatar image) solely to configure your local hunter profile. We do not access your repositories, private emails, or external gaming credentials.

5. Deleting Your Data

To completely erase your data, open the profile picker (the screen shown when you switch profiles or first open Proglog) and use the delete icon next to a profile. This permanently removes that profile's games and trophies from this browser. You can also clear your browser's local site storage for Proglog to remove everything at once.
